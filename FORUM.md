> well, I would build a 10 channel console and use the M523 sub group modules as a master L/R summing buss
>
> Mic / Line ----> Pre-amp ----> EQ send and return in module ----> Insert send and return ----> Direct out to direct out and VU meter ----> M1 and M2 feed the M523 sub groups.
>
> Use the M462B andM466 as patchable sends and returns not in the signal patch.
>
> what kinda of VU meters are they? Vertical? if so use the direct out of the module 
>
> if you have the metal work, it would help. If you need metal work, i MAY have some.
>
> Tones
